Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2 days per month and unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 15 days into the next year.

To request leave, submit a request through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Emergency leave can be requested with same-day notice by informing your manager directly.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive days.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid, and paternity leave is 2 weeks paid. Both must be requested at least 30 days before the expected start date, except in cases of early delivery.

Unused annual leave beyond the carry-forward limit will be forfeited at the end of the calendar year and is not paid out, except upon resignation or termination, where it is compensated at the employee's basic daily pay rate.
