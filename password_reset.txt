Password Reset Procedure

If you forget your company account password, go to the self-service portal at portal.company.com/reset and click "Forgot Password." You will receive a one-time verification code on your registered email or phone number.

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. Passwords cannot be reused from your last 5 passwords.

If you are locked out after 5 failed login attempts, your account will be locked for 30 minutes automatically, or you can contact the IT helpdesk to unlock it immediately.

To reset your password from a company laptop without internet access, hold Ctrl+Alt+Delete and select "Change a password," then follow the on-screen instructions using your current password.

For multi-factor authentication (MFA) issues, such as a lost phone or authenticator app problems, contact the IT helpdesk directly at extension 4357 (HELP) or submit a ticket through the IT service desk portal. Do not share your MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff.
