Benefits Enrollment

New employees have 30 days from their date of joining to enroll in health insurance and other benefit plans through the benefits portal. Failure to enroll within this window means waiting until the next open enrollment period.

Open enrollment happens once a year, typically in November, and allows all employees to add, change, or drop coverage for the following calendar year. Outside of open enrollment, changes can only be made after a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth of a child, or loss of other coverage.

The company offers two health insurance plans: a standard plan fully covered by the employer for the employee, and a premium plan where the employee pays the difference in cost. Both plans can be extended to cover a spouse and up to 3 dependent children at an additional cost deducted from monthly pay.

Life insurance equal to one year's base salary is provided automatically at no cost to all full-time employees. Additional voluntary life insurance can be purchased at group rates during enrollment.

To make changes to your benefits, log into the benefits portal, select "Life Event" or "Open Enrollment," and follow the guided steps. Changes typically take effect on the first day of the following month after submission.
