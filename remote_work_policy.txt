Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval. Roles that require physical presence, such as front-desk or lab-based positions, are not eligible for remote work.

To request a remote work arrangement, submit a request through the HR portal specifying the preferred days and reason. Requests are typically approved or denied within 3 business days.

While working remotely, employees are expected to be reachable during core business hours of 10 AM to 4 PM local time, and to attend all scheduled meetings via video call with camera on unless otherwise agreed.

The company does not provide a stipend for home internet or furniture, but employees can request a one-time equipment loan of a laptop, monitor, and keyboard through the IT asset request form.

Employees working remotely from a different city or country for more than 2 consecutive weeks must inform HR in advance for tax and compliance reasons. Fully remote arrangements (5 days per week) require special approval from the department head and HR and are granted only in exceptional circumstances.
