Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees can claim reimbursement for business-related expenses such as travel, client meals, accommodation, and office supplies purchased out of pocket. All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.

To submit a claim, log in to the expense portal, upload a clear photo or scan of the original receipt, select the correct expense category, and enter a brief business justification. Claims without receipts above 500 rupees will not be approved except in rare pre-approved cases.

Travel expenses for domestic trips are reimbursed based on actual cost for economy class flights or AC 2-tier train travel. International travel requires pre-approval from your manager and finance before booking.

Meal reimbursement while traveling for business is capped at 1200 rupees per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval and are credited directly to the employee's registered bank account. Rejected claims will include a reason and can be resubmitted with corrected information within 15 days.
